<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    The Arequipa City Tour showcases the city's scenic and architectural
    beauty, along with its most remarkable viewpoints. We will visit iconic
    attractions such as Plaza de Armas, one of Peru's most beautiful squares,
    the Church of the Society of Jesus with its cloisters and dome, and the
    Tristán del Pozo mansion.

    We will begin with pickup from your hotel in central Arequipa. Our first
    stop will be Carmen Alto Viewpoint, overlooking the Chilina Valley, before
    continuing to Yanahuara Viewpoint, one of the city's most traditional spots.

    We will then head to the historic center to see its most significant
    architectural monuments. We will visit Santa Catalina Monastery, a
    miniature city that housed Arequipa's nuns for more than 400 years.

    Later, we will continue through Plaza de Armas, surrounded by beautiful
    colonial arches, and admire Arequipa Cathedral from the outside. Finally,
    we will visit the Church and Cloisters of the Society of Jesus, where our
    local guide will share the history of the site.
</section>
