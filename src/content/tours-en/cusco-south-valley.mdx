    Our meeting point will be your hotel reception. Your guide will pick you up at approximately 8:00 a.m.

    We will then head south of Cusco. We will visit Tipón, an Inca ceremonial center, followed by Pikillacta, an ancient walled citadel of the Wari Empire.

    Our final visit is to the colonial Church of Andahuaylillas, also known as the Sistine Chapel of the Americas. Inside, you will find an impressive collection of paintings in gold-leaf frames, murals, Baroque altarpieces and more.

  We will arrive in Cusco at approximately 3:00 p.m.
