<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    We will begin by walking to Qoricancha Temple, one of the Inca Empire's
    most important ceremonial centers, where the supreme deity Inti, the Sun,
    was worshipped.

    We will continue to the Saqsaywaman Archaeological Complex, whose Quechua
    name means “the place where the falcon is satisfied.”

    Our professional guide will share fascinating information about the site
    and allow enough time to explore its interior and take memorable photos.

    We will continue our tour by visiting 3 more archaeological complexes:
    Q’enqo, Puka Pukará and Tambomachay. Each has its own history and unique
    features that will make our visit unforgettable.

  Finally, we will return to Cusco.
</section>
