<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    We will pick up each guest from their hotel between 4:30 and 5:00 a.m.,
    provided it is located around Cusco's historic center.

    From 5:00 to 8:30 a.m., we will travel to the village of Pacchanta.

    From 8:30 to 9:00 a.m., we will enjoy a delicious breakfast. Afterwards,
    we will hike from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. to the beautiful lakes surrounding
    Apu Ausangate: Azulcocha, Otorongo, Oquecocha, Murococha, Pucacocha,
    Alpacocha and Qomercocha.

    We will also admire the Ausangate mountains and the entire Qayangate range.

    Along the way, we will see birds, llamas, alpacas and many vizcachas. With
    some luck, we may also spot the Andean condor.

    After completing the circuit, we will return to Pacchanta to enjoy the
    hot springs.

    From 1:00 to 1:40 p.m., we will enjoy the hot springs, a wonderful way to
    relax our muscles.

    Lunch will be served from 2:00 to 3:00 p.m. After lunch, we will return to
    Cusco between 3:00 and 7:00 p.m.
</section>
