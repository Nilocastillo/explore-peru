<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    Discover Lima on a half-day city tour designed to showcase the best of
    Peru's capital in just 4 hours. Along the way, we will visit Huaca
    Pucllana, a temple with more than 1,500 years of history, and explore
    Lima's historic center, a World Heritage Site.

    We will stroll through Plaza Mayor and Plaza San Martín, where every
    corner preserves part of the history of the City of Kings. We will also
    visit the San Francisco Catacombs and the Central Bank Museum, accompanied
    by passionate guides who will help us understand Lima's cultural wealth.

    The experience is completed by ocean views from Miraflores and Larcomar,
    along with a visit to the peaceful El Olivar park in San Isidro. This is
    a perfect tour for families, curious travelers and anyone wishing to
    begin their cultural adventure in Peru's capital.
</section>
