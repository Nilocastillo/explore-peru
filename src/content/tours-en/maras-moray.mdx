<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    At approximately 08:00, we will begin with pickup from your hotel and a transfer in comfortable tourist transportation.

    Our half-day experience at the Maras salt mines and Moray offers a special opportunity to explore two significant sites: the innovative circular terraces of Moray and the impressive natural salt pans of Maras.

    During the Inca era, these places played crucial roles: Moray served as an agricultural laboratory for acclimatizing seeds, optimizing their distribution across different altitude-based climates, while Maras salt was a valuable trading resource.

    This enriching experience is designed for all ages, immersing us in Inca engineering and economics.

    To conclude our tour, we will visit a traditional textile production center in the picturesque town of Chinchero, where we will discover the art of traditional weaving.

    Our return to Cusco is planned for approximately 14:30, bringing back new knowledge and wonderful memories.
