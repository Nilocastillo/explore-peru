<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    Our meeting point will be your hotel reception. Your guide will pick you up at approximately 7:50 a.m.

    We will then head to the Sacred Valley of the Incas to enjoy its history and archaeological sites.

    Our first visit will be Pisac and its Inca cemetery.

    We will then travel to Urubamba, where you will enjoy a buffet lunch. In the afternoon, we will visit Ollantaytambo Fortress, in one of the towns that still preserves its ancient architecture, Inca streets and local community.

    Our final stop is the town of Chinchero, where we can admire its archaeological park and handicraft centers. Local craftswomen will give us an engaging explanation of how wool is used, dyed and woven.

  Do not forget to pick up some souvenirs.

  Finally, we will board our transportation back to the city of Cusco.
</section>
