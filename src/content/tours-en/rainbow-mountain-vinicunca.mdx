<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    Today we head south of Cusco to admire the incredible shades of Rainbow Mountain, also known as the Mountain of 7 Colors.

    Very early, at around 4:00 a.m., we will pick you up from your hotel. We will set off toward this natural wonder on an approximately 2-hour journey to Cusipata, where we will enjoy a delicious, energizing breakfast typical of the area.

    We will then continue by private transportation to Llacto, the starting point of our hike at 4,000 m above sea level. Before we begin, our team will provide trekking poles and the advice you need to make the most of this experience.

    During the ascent, you can admire alpacas, llamas, wild plants and majestic Andean landscapes, with the imposing snow-capped Ausangate in the background. The hike to the viewpoint takes around 1 hour and 40 minutes, reaching 5,020 m above sea level.

    A unique natural spectacle awaits at the top: the famous Mountain of 7 Colors, whose reddish, green, yellow and turquoise shades seem to come from another planet. You will have time to take photographs, breathe the fresh Andean air and enjoy this magical moment.

    After descending, we will return to Cusipata for a buffet lunch to restore our energy before heading back to the city.

    We will arrive in Cusco at approximately 5:00 p.m., full of joy and the satisfaction of having enjoyed one of Peru's most impressive experiences.
