<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    The tour begins with pickup from your hotel in Cusco and departure south through the South Valley, passing traditional villages and beautiful Andean scenery. Along the way, we stop for a countryside breakfast before continuing toward the district of Checacupe.

    The journey continues along a road surrounded by mountains and rock formations until we reach the trailhead. The easy walk to Palcoyo takes approximately 40 to 50 minutes.

    Along the route, you can admire the three rainbow mountains, the Stone Forest and panoramic views of Apu Ausangate.

    At the viewpoint, you will have free time for photographs and to hear your guide's explanation of the site, its geology and the Andean worldview.

    We will then walk back to our transportation and head to a local restaurant for a traditional lunch.

    Finally, we will travel back to Cusco, where the service ends in the city center.
</section>
