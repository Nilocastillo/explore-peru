<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    The 2-day Inca Trail is a short yet rewarding alternative for travelers
    who want to experience arriving at Machu Picchu on foot along a historic
    route. The journey begins at Km 104 and combines archaeological sites,
    subtropical forest and arrival through Inti Punku, the Sun Gate.

    Along the route, we will visit Chachabamba and Wiñaywayna before enjoying
    our first panoramic view of Machu Picchu. The second day is dedicated to
    a guided visit to the citadel and the return train journey to Ollantaytambo.
</section>
