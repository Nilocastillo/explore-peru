<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    We will begin our journey very early, with pickup in Cusco at 3:00 a.m.,
    followed by an approximately 5-hour drive to the village of Phinaya.

    On arrival, we will enjoy a comforting Andean breakfast, ideal for
    acclimatizing and preparing for the adventure ahead. We will then travel
    for another hour to approach the imposing Quelccaya Suyuparina Glacier,
    recognized as the world's largest tropical glacier.

    From the end of the vehicle route, we will take a short hike of around
    15 to 20 minutes. Along the way, you will witness the area's natural beauty.

    Back in Phinaya, a traditional local lunch prepared with local ingredients
    will be waiting for us.

    We will then board our transportation again to begin the return journey
    to Cusco, making a special stop at magical Sibinacocha Lake, one of the
    largest and most beautiful lakes on the high plateau.

    We will finish near Plaza de Armas between 7:30 and 8:00 p.m.
</section>
