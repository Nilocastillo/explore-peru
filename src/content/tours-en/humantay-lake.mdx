<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    We will pick you up from your hotel very early, at around 5:00 a.m.,
    and travel for almost 2.5 hours toward Humantay Lake.

    We will arrive in Mollepata for a delicious buffet breakfast, then
    continue for another hour to our starting point.

    Once ready, we will begin the ascent to the lake, taking approximately
    1 hour and 40 minutes to reach the highest point.

    The hike is moderately challenging, but the effort is well worth it:
    on arrival, you will discover a true turquoise treasure.

    Once we reach the top, we will have enough time to take photographs and
    enjoy a detailed explanation from our guide.

    After some free time, we will begin our approximately 1.5-hour descent.
    The downhill route will be less demanding, and we will feel rewarded
    after witnessing the beautiful turquoise waters of this enchanting lake.

    In Soraypampa, we will board our return transportation to Mollepata,
    where we will enjoy a delicious lunch to recover our energy and rest
    our legs.

    Afterwards, we will return to Cusco in our private transportation,
    following the same route as at the start of the day. At around 6:00 p.m.,
    the tour will finish with drop-off one block from Plaza de Armas.
</section>
