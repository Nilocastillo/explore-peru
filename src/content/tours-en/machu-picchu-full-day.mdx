<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    We begin with pickup from your hotel and transfer to the bus station. We then board tourist transportation from Cusco to Ollantaytambo to take the train and enjoy an approximately 2-hour journey to Aguas Calientes, also known as the town of Machu Picchu.

    Your guide will be waiting at Aguas Calientes train station to accompany you to the Consettur bus station and travel up to the Inca citadel, a journey of approximately 30 minutes.

    Inside Machu Picchu, you will take an approximately 2-hour guided tour to discover the mysteries of this truly special place.

    You will then return to Aguas Calientes, where you can have lunch at your own expense.

    In the afternoon, you will board the train for your return journey to Cusco. You must be at the station 30 minutes before your train departs. The return follows the same route as the outward journey: upon arrival at Ollantaytambo train station, you will take the same train operator's combined train-and-bus service to Wanchaq station in Cusco.

  Finally, we will transfer you to your hotel.
</section>
