<section id="description">
  <H2Tours>About the experience</H2Tours>

    Enjoy a wonderful experience on a scenic flight over the Nazca Lines in Peru. This tour allows you to discover the fascinating geoglyphs of this ancient world from the air and admire their impressive shapes, created with a single continuous line.

    The Nazca Lines cover an area of approximately 350 km and depict various animals and plants, including the Spider, Monkey, Dog, Lizard, Hummingbird and Condor. Some figures extend between 15 and 300 meters, with lines approximately 30 centimeters deep.
