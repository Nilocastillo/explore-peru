## Policies, terms and conditions

The Peruvian tourism services system may differ from the one in your country. We recommend reading these policies carefully before booking any service with XPLORE PERÚ.

Activity programs are planned with great care. However, operational, weather, logistical or safety reasons may require changes to the order of activities in the itinerary or to the specific route, with advance notice to the customer whenever possible.

These situations are exceptional. Even so, the published itinerary should be considered a basic guide to the service.

During the booked tour, XPLORE PERÚ is not responsible for illnesses contracted, physical or mental disabilities, undisclosed conditions or any personal situation that could endanger the passenger or third parties, or that limits or prevents the provision of the tourism service. The company will not accept claims or provide refunds on these grounds, including cases in which the problem does not appear under normal conditions.

## Passenger responsibilities

Passengers are responsible for meeting all requirements needed for their trip, such as:

- Visas.
- Passports.
- Identity document.
- Current student card or ISIC, where applicable.
- Andean Migration Card, where applicable.
- Vaccinations, if required.
- Authorizations for minors.
- Other documents required by authorities, operators or suppliers.

Failure to meet these requirements may cause delays, service cancellations, payment of taxes, penalties or other difficulties.

Before booking the service, travelers must inform XPLORE PERÚ of any physical, medical, dietary, mental or mobility limitations that could affect the provision of the tourism service. This information allows us to recommend specific services or itineraries better suited to the passenger's condition.

If a passenger is intoxicated or under the influence of substances and poses a risk to themselves or the group, they will not be allowed to take the tour following an assessment by the guide or person responsible for the service. This decision is intended to protect the health and safety of the passenger and other participants.

## Rate policies

All rates are expressed in US dollars unless another currency is indicated, and are calculated per person.

All rates are subject to availability and change.

Children under 2 years and 11 months are considered infants. They do not pay for services, but are not entitled to meals, a bed or a seat on departures unless the supplier states otherwise.

Children from 3 to 10 years and 11 months are considered children and may qualify for a special rate, sharing a room with their parents where applicable.

Those over 11 years of age are considered adults.

Minors must travel with an identity document and the appropriate authorizations when required.

Rates do not apply to festive events or special periods, such as Holy Week, long holiday weekends, Christmas, New Year, Peruvian Independence Day holidays or national celebrations, unless expressly confirmed in writing.

## Booking, deposit and cancellation policies

No booking will be considered valid without written confirmation and payment of the agreed amount by the traveler or intermediary company.

In some special cases, even after a deposit has been paid, the booking must await reconfirmation due to restrictions, availability or regulatory conditions imposed by tourism suppliers.

The final price of tourism services or packages offered by XPLORE PERÚ will be agreed between the customer and the reservations department through the company's official channels.

To arrange a booking, passengers must provide information such as:

- First and last names.
- Passport or identity document number.
- Nationality.
- Date of birth.
- Contact details.

Changes to bookings, itineraries, dates or participants will be accepted subject to availability and payment of the applicable penalties, rate differences and administrative expenses.

For travel packages, the Inca Trail or other alternative routes, a deposit of USD 150.00 per person may be required. The remaining balance may be paid under the conditions communicated by XPLORE PERÚ before the itinerary begins.

Student discounts require a valid copy of a student card or ISIC that is current for the service season. If the corresponding copy is not received with the booking information, the agency will purchase an adult admission ticket.

## Cancellation policy

Customers may cancel their package booking at any time by sending a written request through XPLORE PERÚ's official channels. The following conditions apply according to how far in advance the cancellation is made:

| Advance notice | Penalty for a travel package, Inca Trail or alternative routes |
| --- | --- |
| 30 days or more | Cancellation fee of USD 150.00 |
| 15 to 10 days | Penalty of 60% of the total cost |
| 9 to 7 days | Penalty of 80% of the total cost |
| 6 days or less | Penalty of 100% of the total cost |

## Rescheduling and refunds

Refunds will be made in accordance with the contract or service conditions provided when the sale was finalized.

Preferential rates, promotions, special offers and services on special dates may not be eligible for refunds or changes.

The advance payment of USD 150.00 per person requested for certain services is non-refundable.

Programs on special dates, Holy Week, Peruvian Independence Day holidays, New Year, long holiday weekends, roadblocks, special offers or other high-demand periods will be booked upon advance payment, on a non-refundable basis when specified in the quotation.
