## Privacy policy

At XPLORE PERÚ, we respect the privacy of our customers, passengers and visitors. This policy explains what information we may request, how we use it and the measures we take to protect it while providing our tourism services.

## Information we may collect

To manage bookings, quotations and tourism services, we may request information such as:

- First and last names.
- Identity document or passport.
- Nationality.
- Date of birth.
- Email address and telephone number.
- Information needed for bookings, admissions, tickets, accommodation, transportation or travel-related procedures.
- Special information provided by the passenger, such as dietary restrictions, relevant medical conditions or assistance requirements.

## Use of information

We use personal information to:

- Answer inquiries and send quotations.
- Confirm bookings and coordinate tourism services.
- Arrange admissions, tickets, transportation, accommodation, guides and other suppliers needed to provide the contracted service.
- Provide assistance before, during and after the trip.
- Meet legal, operational or administrative obligations related to providing the service.

## Sharing information with third parties

XPLORE PERÚ may share strictly necessary information with tourism suppliers, operators, hotels, transportation companies, guides, attraction admission authorities or competent authorities when required to provide the contracted service.

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

## Information security

We apply reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. However, no digital transmission or storage system is completely infallible.

## Data retention

Information will be kept for as long as necessary to manage the business relationship, respond to subsequent requests, meet legal obligations or maintain the company's administrative records.

## Rights of the data subject

Passengers may request that their personal data be updated, corrected or deleted, where applicable, by writing to XPLORE PERÚ through its official channels.

## Changes to this policy

XPLORE PERÚ may update this policy to reflect operational, legal or commercial changes. The version published on this website will be the current version.
